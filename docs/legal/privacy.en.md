# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 2026  
**Data controller:** JJ & Co. (Rune Race Team)  
**Website:** [https://rune-race.onrender.com](https://rune-race.onrender.com)

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Rune Race**. JJ & Co. (Rune Race Team) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information when you use our online game.

This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you visit our website or play the game.

## 2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Rune Race, we may process the following:

### 2.1. Email and password accounts

- Email address
- Full name and display name you provide
- Phone number (optional, if you enter it)
- Password stored in hashed form through our authentication provider — we do not store plain-text passwords

### 2.2. Google Sign-In

When you choose **Continue with Google**, we may receive from Google:

- Google account identifier
- Display name
- Email address
- Profile picture (if available)

Your use of Google Sign-In is also governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 2.3. Anonymous (guest) play

You may play without creating a full account. In that case, the system may create an anonymous session to run rooms and multiplayer connections, including a session identifier and temporary display name.

### 2.4. Gameplay and profile data

- In-game display name
- Avatar or emoji you choose (if applicable)
- Play statistics (e.g. games played, wins/losses, play time) for registered accounts
- Multiplayer session data needed to synchronize matches

### 2.5. Technical data

- IP address, browser/device type, and basic error logs for operation, security, and troubleshooting

We do **not** intentionally collect sensitive personal data (e.g. health or financial information).

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:

- Authenticate and manage accounts
- Operate rooms, matchmaking, and multiplayer synchronization
- Display player identity in lobbies and matches
- Store profiles and statistics (for registered accounts)
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and duplicate accounts
- Improve stability, performance, and user experience
- Respond to support requests

We do **not sell or rent** your personal information.

## 4. In-game chat

Rune Race includes real-time chat in rooms/lobbies.

- Messages are delivered between players during the active session
- Messages are **not permanently stored** on our servers
- When a room/lobby ends or is destroyed, related chat content is deleted and **cannot be recovered**

## 5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Information may be shared only when:

- Required by lawful request from competent authorities
- Necessary to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of Rune Race and users
- With service providers that help us operate the game (see Section 9), within the scope of processing on our behalf

## 6. Retention and deletion

- Account information is kept while your account exists or as required by law
- Chat messages are not permanently stored (see Section 4)
- Multiplayer session data may be processed temporarily for synchronization, then discarded when no longer needed

You may contact us for assistance regarding your account or personal data.

## 7. Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect user information. However, no internet transmission system can guarantee 100% absolute security.

## 8. Children's privacy

Rune Race is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the age requiring parental consent under applicable law without appropriate permission.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can assist.

## 9. Third-party services

Rune Race uses the following services to operate:

| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Google Sign-In** | Sign in with a Google account |
| **Supabase** | User authentication, profile storage, and account data |
| **Render** | Hosting the website and game servers |

These providers process data under their own policies. We only share data necessary to deliver the service.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is always published at:

**[https://rune-race.onrender.com/privacy/en](https://rune-race.onrender.com/privacy/en)**

Continued use of Rune Race after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy (to the extent permitted by law).
